# Navigation sound sources

The navigation sound is a free sound effect from Mixkit and is used under the [Mixkit Sound Effects Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree).

- `nav-select-typewriter-hard.wav` — “Hard typewriter click,” Mixkit item [1119](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/download/1119/). Trimmed to its 140 ms transient and converted to mono PCM.
